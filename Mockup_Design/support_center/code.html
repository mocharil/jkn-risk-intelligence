Frequently Asked Questions

View all FAQs

How do I initiate an upcoding investigation workflow? expand_more

Navigate to the 'Claims' view, select a high-risk claim, and click 'Initiate Investigation' in the top action bar. This will move the entity to your Investigation Queue.

What data sources define the baseline for Risk Intelligence scoring? expand_more

Scores are derived from historical institutional claims data cross-referenced against national Medicare/Medicaid benchmarks and known anomaly patterns.

How does the AI Copilot determine confidence intervals? expand_more

Confidence intervals are calculated based on the density of corroborating data points within the provider's peer network graph.

Can I export investigative reports for legal compliance? expand_more

Yes, use the 'Export PDF' function within any active investigation dossier. Ensure you have the requisite PHI handling permissions.
