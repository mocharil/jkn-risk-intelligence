Executive Summary

The investigation into Medika Utama Hospital confirms substantial irregularities consistent with organized upcoding and phantom billing schemes, primarily isolated to the Orthopedic and Cardiology departments between Q1 and Q3 2025.

Cross-referencing claims data with independent medical summaries revealed a systematic pattern of upgrading minor procedures (e.g., standard consultations) to complex interventions (e.g., specialized diagnostic imaging) without supporting clinical indications. Furthermore, patient interviews indicated non-receipt of services billed under 'phantom' codes.
