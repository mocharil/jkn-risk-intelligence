Design a complete, high-fidelity desktop web application for:

JKN RISK INTELLIGENCE

An AI-powered healthcare claim risk detection and investigation platform for Indonesia's national health insurance ecosystem.

The platform helps investigators:

DETECT → PRIORITIZE → INVESTIGATE → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE

The system combines:
- Healthcare claim analytics
- Risk detection
- Anomaly detection
- Provider intelligence
- Network intelligence
- Evidence-based investigation
- Gemini AI reasoning
- AI Copilot
- Data onboarding and schema mapping

IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING:

This is NOT:
- a generic admin dashboard
- a generic BI dashboard
- a CRM
- a banking dashboard
- a hospital management system
- a generic chatbot
- a simple fraud probability classifier

This IS:

A next-generation healthcare risk intelligence and AI investigation platform.

The interface should communicate:

"We do not simply predict suspicious claims.
We help investigators understand WHY something is suspicious,
WHAT evidence supports it,
WHAT related patterns exist,
and WHAT should be investigated next."

The application must feel like a real enterprise product that investigators, analysts, and decision makers could use every day.

43. FINAL DESIGN PRIORITY
==================================================

The interface must be immediately recognizable as a healthcare/JKN risk intelligence product through its WHITE + GREEN institutional visual language.

1. Indonesia Risk Intelligence Map
2. Healthcare Risk Network Graph
3. Evidence-Based Investigation Workspace
4. Gemini-powered AI Intelligence

Prioritize product usability and investigation workflow over visual decoration.

The design should impress in a hackathon presentation while remaining believable as an enterprise healthcare product.

Create production-quality, high-fidelity screens rather than conceptual wireframes.

Use realistic Indonesian healthcare-related sample data and Indonesian Rupiah formatting where appropriate.

Use Bahasa Indonesia for user-facing application labels where natural, while technical terms such as Risk Score, AI Copilot, Upcoding, Phantom Billing, and Network Intelligence may remain in English when clearer.

Most importantly:

DO NOT DESIGN A DASHBOARD WITH AI FEATURES ADDED ON TOP.

DESIGN AN AI-NATIVE HEALTHCARE RISK INVESTIGATION PRODUCT.
